Climatological testbed
Methodology

This experiment evaluates how well reconstruction methods capture the climatological mean state of sea surface CO₂ across different ocean basins.

We compare reconstructed climatologies against observational benchmarks and assess spatial patterns of bias and skill.

Key findings: Mean-state reconstruction skill varies regionally, with systematic biases in certain ocean basins.

Tools: Python, R, climatological analysis, spatial statistics

Spatial bias analysis
Methodology

This component performs basin-scale assessment of spatial biases in climatological reconstructions.

We identify regions where reconstruction methods systematically over- or under-estimate mean-state CO₂ concentrations and investigate underlying causes.

Key findings: Basin-specific bias patterns suggest regional observation gaps and model structural limitations.

Tools: Python, R, spatial bias analysis, basin statistics

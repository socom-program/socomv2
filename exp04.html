Experiment

EXP 04: Climatological testbed

A climatological testbed assessing mean-state sfCO₂ reconstruction skill

Overview

This experiment evaluates the skill of sea surface CO₂ reconstruction methods under conditions of sparse observational coverage. An ensemble of machine learning methods reconstruct a synthetic climatological sfCO₂ field using climatological inputs for feature variables using SOCATv2023 observational coverage.

Description:Experiment 4 attempts to evaluate the skill of sfCO₂ reconstruction methods under a simple testbed condition, and assess the impact of sparse observational coverage. Specifically, the motivation is to answer the question: Is the ocean carbon sink hiatus that many products show in the early 2000s, attributable to sampling alone or is it a true shift in carbon uptake? In this experiment, the true sfCO₂ field has no interannual variability and no internal physical or biogeochemical dynamics. Therefore, the reconstruction aims to identify this prescribed seasonal cycle and long term mean sfCO₂ value only, given available sampling density. There are two sub-experiments conducted: a ‘no-trend’ experiment where the climatology is repeated each year and remains identical each year. Additionally, to more accurately replicate a real-world situation, a ‘with-trend’ reconstruction was also performed, which assigns a 2 microatm per year trend onto the Takahashi climatology sfCO₂ field as well as the atmospheric xCO₂ field. All other variables do not contain an assigned trend.
SOCAT mask used:SOCATv2023 https://socat.info/index.php/version-2023/

Climatological testbed
Methodology

This experiment evaluates how well reconstruction methods capture the climatological mean state of sea surface CO₂ across different ocean basins.

We compare reconstructed climatologies against observational benchmarks and assess spatial patterns of bias and skill.

Key findings: Mean-state reconstruction skill varies regionally, with systematic biases in certain ocean basins.

Tools: Python, R, climatological analysis, spatial statistics

Spatial bias analysis
Methodology

This component performs basin-scale assessment of spatial biases in climatological reconstructions.

We identify regions where reconstruction methods systematically over- or under-estimate mean-state CO₂ concentrations and investigate underlying causes.

Key findings: Basin-specific bias patterns suggest regional observation gaps and model structural limitations.

Tools: Python, R, spatial bias analysis, basin statistics

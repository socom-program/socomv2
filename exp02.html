Experiment

EXP 02: Multiple hindcast testbed

Assesses how sparse SOCAT sampling affects sfCO₂ reconstructions and the ocean carbon sink.

Overview

sfCO₂ reconstruction methods are trained on sparse sfCO₂ observations, so their skill in unsampled regions cannot be tested against the real ocean. This experiment uses GOBMs as a known model truth to assess how well each sfCO₂ reconstruction method reproduces sfCO₂, FCO₂ and the ocean carbon sink.

Methodology

This experiment evaluates 9 sfCO₂ reconstruction methods against 5 GOBMs, each subsampled to SOCATv2023.

We assess how the choice of GOBM and sfCO₂ reconstruction method affects the accuracy and uncertainty of the sfCO₂ reconstructions, FCO₂ and the ocean carbon sink.

Key findings: The skill of the sfCO₂ reconstructions varies considerably across GOBMs and sfCO₂ reconstruction methods.

Tools: Python, R, statistical analysis, model intercomparison

Methodology

The sfCO₂ reconstructions are compared with the GOBM truth using bias, RMSE and correlation, per region.

This shows which sfCO₂ reconstruction methods perform best in which ocean regions, and how robust that is across GOBMs.

Key findings: No single sfCO₂ reconstruction method performs best across all GOBMs; performance depends on the region and the availability of sfCO₂ observations.

Tools: Python, R, performance metrics, statistical analysis

Description:Experiment 3 evaluates reconstruction bias of four independent data products by using a testbed of six CMIP6 Earth System Models (ESMs). 18 ESM ensemble members are sub-sampled based on the spatiotemporal coverage in SOCATv2024, and surface ocean pCO2 is reconstructed globally over the period of 1983-2023 using the algorithms of the four data products. ESMs capture a range of potential surface ocean pCO2 states in the ocean system by providing multiple ensemble members. This experiment expands on Experiment 2, where reconstruction skill is assessed using five GOBMs as a testbed. For Experiment 3 we ask: Is reconstruction bias of individual data products consistent and robust across model structures and internal variability? .
SOCAT mask used:SOCATv2024 https://socat.info/index.php/version-2023/

Large ensemble testbed
Methodology

This experiment employs an 18-member CMIP6 Earth System Model ensemble to capture diverse climate states and evaluate reconstruction method robustness.

We assess how reconstruction methods perform across different climate regimes and identify systematic biases or dependencies on climate state.

Key findings: Large ensembles reveal significant climate-state dependencies in reconstruction performance.

Tools: Python, R, CMIP6 data analysis, ensemble statistics

Methodology

This component analyzes ensemble variability across different climate states to identify robust reconstruction methods.

We quantify the sensitivity of reconstruction performance to climate variability and develop climate-adaptive reconstruction strategies.

Key findings: Ensemble-based approaches improve robustness across diverse climate conditions compared to single-model methods.

Tools: Python, R, ensemble statistics, climate analysis

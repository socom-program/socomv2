Geospatial uncertainty
Methodology

This experiment quantifies how uncertainties in regional ocean carbon flux estimates propagate to global scale estimates through spatial aggregation.

We analyze the impact of observational sparsity and reconstruction method choice on the uncertainty budget at different spatial scales.

Key findings: Regional uncertainties can significantly affect global flux estimates, particularly in data-sparse regions.

Tools: Python, R, statistical analysis, uncertainty quantification

Spatial uncertainty mapping
Methodology

This component focuses on mapping spatial uncertainty patterns across different ocean regions and identifying hotspots of uncertainty.

We develop a regional analysis framework to quantify uncertainty contributions from different sources at various spatial scales.

Key findings: Spatial uncertainty patterns reveal systematic regional biases that require targeted observation strategies.

Tools: Python, R, spatial statistics, uncertainty mapping
